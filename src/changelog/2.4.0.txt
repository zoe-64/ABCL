This is the release of the beta 2.3.14 - 2.4.0, if you tested the beta then you may recognize most changes.
Thank you to all who participated. 

Release 2.4.0:
Replaced point-and-click minigame with a more interesting minigame
Added an option to switch back to the old minigame
Added minigame difficulty option

Made changing diapers require a piece of furniture like a changing table for immersion
Made bladder and bowels limited to 100 percent and going beyond that will cause an accident
Made bladder and bowel messages more vague and less technical
Lowered diaper capacity for messes
Added particles to show diaper state

Made an option to auto-unpause when you have a diaper
Adjusted the diaper verbs to be more appropriate

Added crafting properties (requires device level on FUSAM to save properly):
Discoloration Protection - protects diapers from changing color when wet or messy
Hollow - normal plugs become hollow

Added a command to make suggestions in game:
/abcl-wish message (up to 4000 characters)

Fixes:
Fixed or attempted to fix an infinite loop that would update the player's appearance repeatedly
Fixed rub front not really working correctly
Fixed inconsistency with remote settings
Fixed an issue where everyone was stuck at 80% regression~
Fixed issue where baby could pause stats when they didn't have permission
Fixed removing all diapers not taking away mess and wetness
